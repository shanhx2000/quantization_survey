   Regarding the precision range, we observed that extending
the maximum precision beyond 4 bits requires assigning
a larger portion of ranks to low-precision regions to meet
the target Bavg , which offsets the benefit of using higher
precision. We also exclude 0-bit allocation, i.e., pruning,
because reducing a component from (n + 1) to n bits only
lowers its representational capacity, whereas assigning 0 bits
represents a total loss of information. This helps explain why
rank-truncation methods often suffer from larger accuracy
